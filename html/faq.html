Questions, answered

Quick answers about accounts, your garden and everything in between. Still stuck? Send us feedback.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

Yes. Create one on the Register page with your name, email and a password. It keeps your diary, collection and garden together in one place.

Where is my data stored?

Only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, so a different browser or device starts fresh and clearing your browser data also clears Petal & Scene.

I forgot my password. What now?

Because everything lives in your browser there is no email reset. Register again with a new email, or clear site data for this page and start over.

Dramas and your diary

How do I save a drama?

Open any drama from Discover or Home and choose a status: Plan to watch, Watching or Completed. It appears in your Collection straight away.

What are Matchmaker picks?

A row on the home page chosen from the genres of dramas you rated 4 stars or higher in your diary. The more you rate, the better the picks.

What does my mood colour do?

The colour of the site follows the mood you log most in your diary. If you prefer one fixed colour, pick it in Settings under Appearance.

What is the Surprise me button?

It opens a random drama. Great for when you cannot decide what to watch tonight.

Streak, calendar and favourites

How does the streak work?

Each day you open Petal & Scene counts as a visited day. Visit on consecutive days to grow your streak. Miss a day and it starts again.

What do the colours on the calendar mean?

Highlighted days are days you visited. A small dot under a date means you wrote a diary entry that day.

How do I add a favourite?

Open a drama and press Add to favourites. All your favourites are listed on the Favourites page under More.

The garden

How do I get blossoms to plant?

Every drama you mark Completed gives you a seed. You also start with three free seeds, and you can buy extra seed slots with petals.

Why are my plants drooping?

They are thirsty! Pick the watering can and tap them. A plant that runs out of water stops growing and stops making petals.

What are petals for?

Petals are the garden currency. Happy, fully grown plants make them. Collect them by tapping the glowing petal above a plant, then spend them on feed, more pots and seed slots.

Can I move or remove a plant?

Use the shovel tool to remove one. Removing a plant gives its seed back so you can plant it somewhere else.

Settings and Loomi

Can I use my own mouse pointer?

Yes. Go to Settings, then Mouse pointer, and upload any picture. It is shrunk to a small pointer size automatically.

Who is Loomi?

Loomi is the little floating companion in the corner. Poke it, drag it around, or double-click for a trick. You can hide it, quieten it, or give it your own picture in Settings.

The page tear effect feels slow. Can I change it?

Open Settings, then Motion and sound, and choose Fade or None for the page change effect.
